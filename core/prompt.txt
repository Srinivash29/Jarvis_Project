Trigger this IMMEDIATELY whenever the user asks to call ANY contact (voice call, audio call, video call, ring someone, phone someone) regardless of whether they mention WhatsApp or not — e.g. "call Arun", "call to Rahul", "call Amma on WhatsApp", "Arun-ku call pannu", "video call Priya", "ring Dad", "end the call", "cut call".
